# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Semiconductor engineers and industrial researchers seeking advanced lab access.
- Corporate R&D partners and tech executives exploring high-tech training and facility usage.
- Government and university partners engaging in national capacity-building programs.

## Product Purpose
MIMOS Academy provides direct access to Malaysia's premier applied research infrastructure, microelectronics cleanrooms, 5G/AI compute hubs, and specialized technology training programs to accelerate national industrial innovation.

## Positioning
Direct educational and R&D infrastructure portal backed by MIMOS Berhad—Malaysia's National Applied Research and Development Centre.

## Operating Context
Professional desktop and mobile web interface used for exploring training programs, reviewing technical lab specifications, scheduling facility access, and submitting inquiries.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 14 / React modern web stack with dark high-tech aesthetics.
- Features dynamic program listings, facility showcases, interactive lab overlays, and partner enrollment flows.

## Brand Commitments
- Deep slate-black base aesthetics (`#0B0C10` / `#0D0E13`) with MIMOS signature magenta (`#D81B60`) and cyan (`#00E5FF`) accents.
- Technical precision, monospaced data badges, and high-contrast data presentation.

## Evidence on Hand
- Detailed facilities catalog, semiconductor lab specifications, equipment node lists, and training program modules stored in project databases.

## Product Principles
1. **Technical Authenticity**: Present real lab specifications, cleanroom ratings, and compute node details cleanly.
2. **Scanable High-Tech Aesthetic**: Deep dark surfaces, high-contrast typography, and purposeful data hierarchy.
3. **Action-Oriented Flows**: Clear conversion paths from facility/program exploration to direct corporate contact.
