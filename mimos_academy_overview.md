# Overview of MIMOS Academy

**MIMOS Academy** is the strategic technology capability development and talent solutions organization operating within **MIMOS Berhad**—Malaysia’s National Applied Research and Development Centre under the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MOSTI).

---

## 🎯 Core Purpose & Mission

MIMOS Academy bridges the critical gap between academic theory, deep-tech research and development (R&D), and real-world industrial demands. By leveraging MIMOS's rich R&D heritage and ecosystem, the Academy acts as a catalyst for Malaysia's digital transformation agenda and strategic technology talent development.

---

## 💡 Key Pillars & Capabilities

### 1. Future-Ready Talent Development
* **Professional Certification Programs:** High-impact technical training programs designed for engineers, IT professionals, and technical leaders.
* **Specialized Curricula:** Custom workforce development programs built in collaboration with corporate enterprises and government agencies.
* **HRD Corp Claimable:** High-value training structured to align with national workforce skill initiatives.

### 2. High-Tech Sectors & Focus Areas
* **Semiconductor & Microelectronics:** Wafer fabrication processes, microchip design, and testing.
* **Artificial Intelligence & Big Data:** Applied AI models, data analytics, and high-performance computing.
* **Cybersecurity & Cyber Range Operations:** Advanced threat detection, vulnerability analysis, and network defense.
* **Smart Manufacturing & IoT:** Industrial automation, sensor networks, and 5G application environments.

### 3. World-Class Shared Infrastructure
* Direct access to Malaysia’s premier national applied R&D laboratories, industrial wafer fab cleanrooms, 5G/AI compute hubs, and calibration facilities at the MIMOS headquarters.

### 4. Enterprise Solutions & Technology Consultancy
* Technical advisory and consultancy for enterprise digital transformation.
* Collaborative R&D project management and technology adoption frameworks for industry partners.

---

## 🌐 Vision & Impact

> *"Architecting Malaysia's Strategic Technology Talent."*

MIMOS Academy aims to be recognized as a national leader in technology talent development and innovation, positioning Malaysia as a premier regional hub for technological excellence and deep-tech capabilities.
